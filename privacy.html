Privacy Policy

Impact X · Last updated: 2 November 2026

Short version: you can play as a guest and we collect nothing about you. If you make an account, we store only a username, a scrambled (hashed) password and your game progress. No email, no real name, no ads, no tracking.

1. Who we are

Impact X ("we", "us") is a free educational browser game about the UN Global Goals. Contact: [add your contact email here]

2. What we collect

Guest play. Nothing is sent to us. Your progress (sparks, levels, skins, settings) is stored only inside your own browser (local storage), encrypted on your device.

Accounts (optional). If you create an account we store: your username; your password as a salted scrypt hash (we cannot read it); a hashed recovery code; the date you accepted these policies; session records so you stay signed in; and your game progress (sparks, levels completed, skins owned and equipped, simple settings).

Technical data. Our hosting provider (Netlify) automatically processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and request times to deliver the site and keep it secure. We also use your IP address briefly for abuse limits (for example, limiting login attempts).

What we do not collect: your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, payment details, or advertising and analytics data. We do not sell data and we show no ads.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use one essential, HttpOnly cookie (ix_s) to keep you signed in when you have an account. The game also uses your browser's local storage to keep your saved progress and a few settings. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

4. Other services

The game is hosted by Netlify, which also stores account data. The 3D graphics library is loaded from the cdnjs content network (Cloudflare), which can see your IP address when it serves the file. Voice lines use your own browser's built-in speech feature, which runs on your device or your browser provider's service.

5. How we use data

Only to run the game: sign you in, save and load your progress, prevent abuse and keep the service secure.

6. How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. You can delete it yourself in the game (profile button, then Delete account), which removes your account and online save. Accounts that stay unused for a long time may be removed.

7. Security

We use HTTPS, salted password hashing, HttpOnly session cookies, login rate limits and server-side checks on saved data. No online service is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password and keep your recovery code safe. Because we hold no email address, we cannot recover an account without the password or recovery code.

8. Children

Guests of any age can play without giving us any information. Accounts are for people aged 13 or over, or younger players whose parent or guardian allows it where the law requires. If you believe a child has created an account without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. You can delete your account in the game at any time. For any other request, contact us at the address above.

10. International transfers

Our hosting provider may process data in countries other than your own.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use after a change means you accept it.
